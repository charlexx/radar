Efuru and the Woman of the Lake

Past  ·  7 March 2026 – 28 March 2026

Venue

ADEGBOLA Gallery (gallery)

Lagos, Nigeria

Artists

  • Ladi Kwali Nigeria
  • Nengi Omuku Nigeria

Drawing from Flora Nwapa's 1966 novel Efuru, celebrates women artists from the 1940s to the present. Features Ladi Kwali, Peju Alatise, Nengi Omuku, Ndidi Emefiele, and others. Curated by Adekepemi Aderemi.
