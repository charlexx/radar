A World in Common: Contemporary African Photography

Past  ·  1 January 2026 – 30 June 2026

Venue

C/O Berlin (institution)

Berlin, Germany

Artists

  • Atong Atem South Sudan
  • Sammy Baloji Democratic Republic of the Congo
  • James Barnor Ghana
  • Zohra Opoku Ghana

Twenty-three African and African diaspora artists from different generations and geographical contexts. Over one hundred works challenging western-oriented conceptions, exploring alternative historical narratives rooted in diverse experiences of the African continent. Three chapters covering cultural heritage, spirituality, self-representation, and climate justice.
